The terminal dashboard collects your quota readings itself and shows **Usage**, **History**, **Settings** and **Logs**
in any terminal. It needs no desktop app and runs on Linux, macOS and Windows.

Start with [installation](/tui/install/), then learn the [interface](/tui/interface/). The
[shared reference](/reference/) explains quota readings, History and settings used across frontends.
